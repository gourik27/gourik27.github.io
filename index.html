Reflection on Unit 2

In Unit 2, The Nature of Information Problems, we examined how digital images represent information through pixel intensity values. We worked with a grayscale image and visualized its pixel structure to understand how images are composed numerically. A histogram of pixel intensities was plotted to analyze the distribution of brightness levels within the image. We then increased the brightness and generated a new histogram to observe changes in intensity distribution. In the laboratory session, we applied edge detection techniques,including the extraction of horizontal edges and image blurring. Additionally, we performed image rotation at different angles to study geometric transformations and their effects on image representation. We learnt to estimate Video size.
