Reflection on Unit 2

Understanding the Nature of information problems through digital images.

In Unit 2, The Nature of Information Problem, I understood how images can be treated as data and analyzed computationally. Before this unit, I usually thought of images simply as pictures. But through classes and lab activities I realized that images are actually made up of pixels, and each pixels has a numerical intensity value. Working with grayscale images helped me understand this idea clearly as grayscale images do not contain colors but only different level of brightness. We did edge detection, extracting horizontal edges, blurring the images and rotating the images by different degrees. This unit changed the way I think of images. Instead of only seeing them visually, I started to see them as structured information that can be analyzed and tranformed using computational techniques.I learned Optimisation,Modelling, abstarction and bridges of Konigsberg, Modeling an elevator. In labs and classes we solved many problems related to optimization and Solved problems related to logustucs network optimization, Knapsack problems by doing these all i improved myself in information field . This unit helped me better understand how information problems can be approached using data analysis methods.
