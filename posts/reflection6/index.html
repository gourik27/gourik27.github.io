Reflection on the Impact of Artificial Intelligence and Automation

May 8, 2026

From the first group’s presentation on “AI and Automation”, I got to know that these are not just technologies, but also the things which are changing our daily lives and future careers. I thought that AI is just about robots which are replacing humans but by this presentation I understood that AI is helping people by making work faster, easier and productively. The main point I understood is that understanding AI differs from automation because automation just follows the established rules while AI has the ability to learn and make its own conclusions.

The other important point that I understood is that the fear of technology is not anything new. People were afraid to lose their jobs during both the Industrial Revolution and computerization, but even after some jobs were lost some new job positions appeared. This shows that AI is likely to transform employment rather than take away people from jobs completely.

Another point I understood from the presentation was that the use of technology is always determined by society. It is the governments, businesses, and individuals that dictate how technology can be utilized. This shows that humans have control on AI and it is not a thing that works on its own. The concept of “Human + AI collaboration” interested me as AI is helping people not replacing them.

Also, the topic made me think about the importance of responsibility and ethics. AI creates opportunities, while at the same time it creates gaps in inequality if it is not used properly. I thought that it is important to learn how technologies work technically, along with how it affects people, society, jobs, and daily life. This presentation changed my way of thinking about AI from thinking about it negatively to AI as a good tool which creates positive change if humans use it responsibly and properly.
