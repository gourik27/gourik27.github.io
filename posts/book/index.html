The Main Idea of the lesson is Situation Awareness which means underastanding the kind of situation we are in so we can respond correctly and avoid unnecessary failure. This chapter was very insightful and i felt it is easy to understand because author used real life examples and simple classifications. Author divided situations into consistent, variable and novel types made by this the topic was very clear . After reading this chapter I realized that we should not treat every failure in the same way instead we should think about the which type of situation we were in.

I learned that we have to adjust our behaviour based on the situation . For example in routine tasks I should be more disciplined and careful to avoid unnecessary mistakes. We often blame ourselves or others immediately when something goes wrong but from this chapter I learnt failure is not due to lack of ability but because situation will be complex or new . If we are trying new for example learning a dance step in initial it will be difficult and face many failures due to that is new after more practice we won’t fail. So trying new and failing is a learning not failure.

Another thing I learned is that idea of an “accident waiting to happen” very interesting. Author explained that in places like hospitals or team environments , small issues like not speaking up with people or misunderstanding instructions can turn into bigger failure if ignored. It is very important in group works. So I realized that problems can be avoided if we notice ealry warnings signs like confusions, poor communication.

As now I know about situation awarness, I will start applying it in my real life. This chapter changed my view on failure and my way of treating failure. However failures are common in everyones life we should not get depressed instead we have look at situation and try our best until we win. By knowing the situation wheather it is routine , uncertain, or new can help us make better decisions and learn from our mistakes.
