Reflection On Failure

February 18, 2026

Understanding Failure through Context and Consequences and Case study Y2K Bug.

Normally I thought Failure is my fault , I did mistake always. But after reading the lesson Context and Consequences from the book Right Kind of Wrong : The Science of Failing Well written by Amy C. Edmondson. Most important thing I learned from this chapter is that situation matters and we should be aware of the situation whicjh we are in and respond accordingly. According to the Author Different contexts different consequences so situation matters. And this lesson changed my way of treating failure.

From Y2K Bug I got to know that how a small technical issue can create huge global problem. To save memory in olden days programmers set computers to show the year as two digit like 1999 as 99 but when the year changed from 1999 to 2000 computers read it as 1900 instead of 2000, this created huge problem. This problem is connected with The Idea of “accident waiting to happen " with real life. This made me realize that many problems are not sudden but due to the lack of awareness.

Overall, what I learned from these two things is we should be aware os situation and handle it carfully instead of blaming ourselves or others immediately.
